## Proposed open-universe direction

Moonnet Saga is considering a future transition to **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)** for materials whose rights are fully controlled by Yoshie Yamada.

The goal is not simply to make the texts free to read. The larger idea is to make the Moonnet Saga a reusable fictional commons that can continue to branch through human and AI creativity.

Under the proposed direction, people would be welcome to:

- create fan fiction, spin-offs, translations, games, visual works, audio works, and other derivative creations;
- remix and extend settings, characters, technologies, histories, and unexplored branches of the universe;
- use the material commercially as permitted by CC BY-SA 4.0;
- let AI systems read or reference the material as context, build lorebooks or structured world data from it, and use it as a foundation for new creative branches, subject to applicable law and license obligations;
- redistribute and adapt open materials while preserving attribution and ShareAlike requirements.

The repository may eventually include machine-readable worldbuilding resources such as timelines, character relations, terminology, lorebooks, JSON/YAML data, or other formats intended for both humans and AI systems.

### Canon and branches

Opening the universe does **not** mean that every derivative work becomes official canon.

- Works and settings explicitly designated by Yoshie Yamada remain the official Moonnet Saga canon.
- Anyone may create independent branches and derivative works under the applicable license.
- A derivative branch may remain independent even if it contradicts canon.
- Inclusion into official canon remains an editorial decision by Yoshie Yamada unless a future policy explicitly says otherwise.

In Git-like terms: **forking is welcome; merging into canon is selective.**

### Scope and transition

This is currently a **proposal, not an active license change**.

Before adopting CC BY-SA 4.0, the repository and source works must be reviewed so that only materials for which Yoshie Yamada holds the necessary rights are included. Third-party illustrations, quotations, commissioned materials, contractual publications, or other content with separate rights may require exclusion or separate treatment.

If and when the transition is made, the effective date, covered works, exclusions, attribution format, and license notice will be stated explicitly in this repository.

A voluntary support link (for example, a coffee / Ko-fi button) may coexist with the open license. Access and reuse would not depend on payment.

## 将来のオープン化構想

将来的には、山田佳江が必要な権利を単独で保有している作品・設定資料等について、**Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International（CC BY-SA 4.0）** を基本ライセンスとして公開する方向を検討しています。

目的は単に「無料で読める小説」にすることではありません。Moonnet Saga を、人間とAIの双方が読み継ぎ、使い、分岐させ、新しい物語へ育てられる **fictional commons / 共有創作宇宙** として開くことを構想しています。

想定している利用には、たとえば次のようなものがあります。

- 二次創作、スピンオフ、翻訳、ゲーム、映像、音声、イラスト等の制作
- 世界設定、人物、技術、歴史、未観測領域を使った独自 branch の創作
- CC BY-SA 4.0の条件に従った商用利用
- AIへのコンテキスト投入・参照、Lorebook化、構造化データ化、AIを用いた派生創作
- 人間とAIの双方が扱いやすい年表、人物関係、用語、JSON/YAML等の公開

### Canonとbranch

オープン化しても、すべての派生作品が公式Canonになるわけではありません。

- 山田佳江が公式作品・公式設定として指定したものを、Moonnet Saga のCanonとします。
- 他の創作者は、ライセンスの範囲内で自由に独自branchを作れます。
- Canonと矛盾するbranchが存在してもかまいません。
- branchを公式Canonへ取り込むかどうかは、別途方針を定めない限り山田佳江が判断します。

Git的にいえば、**forkは歓迎、Canonへのmergeは選択的**という考え方です。
